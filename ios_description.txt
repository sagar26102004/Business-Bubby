WHY THESE:
  - Apple indexes APP NAME + SUBTITLE + KEYWORDS together. Repeating a word
    from any of them wastes characters, so "one", "place", "local", "shop" and
    "service" are deliberately absent — they are already covered by the name
    and subtitle above.
  - All singular. Apple matches plurals automatically; typing both wastes space.
  - No spaces after commas. A space costs a character and buys nothing.
  - "tiffin" is a deliberate India-market term with low competition.
  - If you change the SUBTITLE, revisit this list — the overlap rule breaks.

SECTION 4 — DESCRIPTION  (4000 char max)
===============================================================================
3449 / 4000

One Place brings your whole neighbourhood into a single app. Find the shops, services, rentals and everyday sellers around you — then order, book, message or call them directly.

BROWSE WHAT IS ACTUALLY NEAR YOU
Every listing is sorted by how far it is from where you are standing. Filter by what you need — food, health, home services, rentals, electronics and more — and see the distance to each place before you tap.

DEALS AROUND THE CORNER
Live offers from businesses near you, right on the home screen. No coupon hunting.

SEARCH THAT UNDERSTANDS SHOPS
Search by business name, by what they sell, or by the exact dish, product or service you want. Suggestions appear as you type.

SEE IT ON A MAP
Open the map to see everything plotted around you, with distance rings, and tap any marker to jump straight to that business.

ORDER, BOOK AND ENQUIRE
Send an order for products or services and get a real answer back — accepted, or a proposal with anything unavailable removed and the total adjusted. Book appointments with service providers. Eating in? Your order becomes a running tab you can add rounds to until you ask for the bill.

TALK WITHOUT GIVING OUT YOUR NUMBER
Message any business in the app, or place a voice call over the internet. No phone number is exchanged in either direction.

RATINGS YOU CAN TRUST
Only verified customers can rate a business — someone who actually ordered, booked or was billed. Low ratings must include a written reason. No paid reviews, no rating from someone who never walked in.

SELL YOUR OWN THINGS
Got a phone, a bike or a bookshelf to sell? Open a stall. Everything you list lives together under your name instead of scattering into separate listings. Add photos from your camera or gallery, set a price, and answer questions on a public thread where the next buyer can read what you already told the last one.

RENT THINGS OUT
List a flat, a car, a bike or furniture with a clear per day or per month price.

RUN YOUR BUSINESS FROM YOUR PHONE
Listing is free and takes a few minutes — one question per screen, no forms to wrestle with. Then manage it all in one workspace:
• Take and answer orders, and issue bills
• Reply to customer messages, and pick who on your team can
• Accept or decline appointment bookings
• Keep a customer list built automatically from everyone you have dealt with
• Run recurring plans — gym memberships, tuition batches, a school bus seat
• Add managers and staff with the access you choose
• Promote an offer to people nearby
• Show off past work in a photo and video showcase
• Print a QR code customers can scan to reach your page

LIVE VEHICLE TRACKING
Running a school van, a delivery bike or a goods vehicle? Drivers switch on live sharing for a shift, and the owner — plus the parents or customers whose children or goods are on board — can watch it move on the map. It is off by default and the driver controls it.

LOOK AROUND WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
Browse everything, search and open the map without signing up. Create an account only when you want to order, message, review or list something.

NO PAYMENTS, NO SURPRISES
One Place does not take payments or commission. You deal with the business directly, exactly as you would in person.

BUILT FOR INDIAN NEIGHBOURHOODS
Prices in rupees, made for the way local markets actually work — the tailor, the tiffin service, the tyre shop and the person two streets away selling a second-hand fridge, all in one place.

SECTION 12 — BLOCKERS. DO NOT SUBMIT UNTIL THESE ARE RESOLVED.
===============================================================================

BLOCKER 1 — NO IN-APP ACCOUNT DELETION
  Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v). Automatic rejection.
  Apps that let users create an account MUST let them delete it FROM INSIDE THE
  APP. An email address or a web form is NOT sufficient for Apple (Google Play
  does accept a web URL; Apple does not).
  Current state: src/app/(tabs)/account.tsx offers only sign in, create account
  and sign out. There is no deleteAccount anywhere in the codebase.
  Fix: prompt P09 in prompt.txt.

BLOCKER 2 — NO REPORT OR BLOCK FOR USER-GENERATED CONTENT
  Apple Guideline 1.2. Automatic rejection.
  Any app with user-generated content must provide ALL FOUR of:
      a) a method for filtering objectionable material
      b) a mechanism to REPORT offensive content, with timely responses
      c) the ability to BLOCK abusive users
      d) published contact information
  This app has (d) only — the support page. A codebase search for report /
  block / moderation tooling returns nothing.
  Affected surfaces: reviews, customer chat, business-to-business chat, public
  question-and-offer threads on stall items, business listings, stall photos.
  Fix: not yet written as a prompt. Needs a report action on reviews, messages
  and listings; a block-user capability; and a moderation route for you to act
  on reports. Add it to prompt.txt as P12 before submitting.

BLOCKER 3 — REVIEWER WILL SEE AN EMPTY APP
  Apple Guideline 2.1 (App Completeness).
  App Review operates from California. All seed data is in Indore. A location
  based directory with nothing nearby looks like a broken or incomplete app.
  Mitigation already applied: the location instruction is the FIRST thing in
  the review notes, in capitals. Reviewers do sometimes skip notes.
  Stronger fix: seed a handful of demo listings near Cupertino (37.3349,
  -122.0090) so the app is never empty regardless of what the reviewer does.

BLOCKER 4 — NO APPLE DEVELOPER ACCOUNT
  $99/yr, and identity verification takes days to weeks. Nothing can be
  submitted without it. Start this FIRST — it is pure waiting time and it
  gates everything else, including the push certificates.

BLOCKER 5 — APP ICON USES THIRD-PARTY IP
  Apple Guideline 5.2 (Intellectual Property).
  The icon pairs "OP" with Luffy's straw hat from One Piece, a Shueisha/Toei
  property. Apple rejects for IP infringement, and rights holders in this
  space are active. Commission an original mark before submitting; it is far
  cheaper than a rejection and resubmission cycle.
