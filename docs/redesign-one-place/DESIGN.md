## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a warm, tactile, neighborhood-first marketplace and business workspace. It departs from sterile, corporate software and heavy utilitarian dashboards in favor of an organic, artisanal paper-and-linen foundation paired with grounded, earthy hues.

The design movement merges **Tactile Organic Minimalism** with **Warm Craft Editorial**. Surfaces mimic unbleached natural paper and linen, while cards echo neatly laid-out neighborhood shop circulars, physical receipts, and physical index cards.

- **Personality:** Dependable, neighborly, grounded, tactile, and energetic.
- **Target Audience:** Local business proprietors, artisans, stall owners, service providers, and neighborhood shoppers who value community proximity and authentic local commerce.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires trust, calm efficiency, neighborly connection, and tangible ownership over one's daily work and trade.

## Colors

The palette establishes an earthy, print-inspired visual structure. It pairs organic grounding tones with decisive action accents:

- **Primary (`#2D5A43` Deep Sage Green):** The institutional anchor. Used for primary interactive triggers, active tab pills, selected segmented switches, and verified identity badges.
- **Secondary (`#E06D53` Warm Terracotta):** High-conversion accent that radiates kinetic warmth. Reserved for calls-to-action ("Order Now", "Book Now"), flash deals, live notices, and promotional banners.
- **Tertiary (`#F0A500` Star Gold):** Dedicated to verified review scores, vendor accolades, and marketplace star ratings.
- **Neutral (`#1E2922` Dense Charcoal):** Deep organic ink color providing crisp contrast without the sterile harshness of pitch black.
- **Base Surfaces & Canvas:** The background canvas relies on Linen Paper (`#FBF9F5`), elevated by crisp Surface White (`#FFFFFF`) cards and framed by Warm Subtle Stone borders (`#E8E4DC`). Secondary control tracks utilize Soft Linen Wash (`#F2EFEB`).
- **Semantic Fills:** Operational status indicators use Soft Mint (`#DCFCE7`) with Forest Green text (`#15803D`) for open businesses, while Urgent Amber (`#D97706`) and Danger Crimson (`#DC2626`) signal workspace alerts and cancellations.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to project an approachable, friendly, yet structurally dependable character. Its generous x-height and clear geometric apertures guarantee high legibility in outdoor daylight and compact handheld screens.

- **Display & Headings:** Keep tight line-height multipliers (1.2× to 1.25×) to anchor compact multi-line business names and storefront titles without unnecessary line gaps.
- **Body Text:** Uses a relaxed 1.5× line height with Charcoal `#1E2922` to ensure effortless reading for long merchant bios, terms, and invoice summaries.
- **Labels & Captions:** Emphasize medium and bold weights (`500` and `700`) to guarantee instant scan speed across status pills, category pills, and transactional price tags.
- **Secondary Tone:** Stone Olive (`#637067`) handles auxiliary metadata, operational hours, distance indicators, and sub-labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is driven by a strict 4px/8px modular rhythm, supporting an adaptive fluid grid tailored for high-density local commerce and workspace task switching.

- **Mobile Viewports (< 640px):** Single-column feed or dual-column stall grid with strict 16px (`1rem`) outer margins and 16px gutters. Tap zones strictly enforce minimum 48px heights.
- **Tablet Viewports (640px – 1024px):** 2-column business listings and 3-to-4-column product grids with 24px section margins.
- **Desktop Viewports (> 1024px):** Multi-column structured workspace layout bounded by a maximum readable width of 1400px for transactional feeds and 1800px for large stall grids.
- **Vertical Spacing:** Modules and major sections separate cleanly by 24px (`space-xl`) or 32px to provide breathing room on the natural linen canvas without disjointing related operational modules.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids dark, heavy, synthetic drop shadows. Depth and hierarchy are achieved through a disciplined combination of **Tonal Paper Layers** and **Low-Contrast Stone Outlines**:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Linen Paper (`#FBF9F5`) acts as the natural unbleached desk surface.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Containers):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) floating cards, framed with a uniform 1px solid border in Warm Stone (`#E8E4DC`). This creates an immediate physical card edge akin to high-grade stationery.
- **Layer 2 (Header & Navigation Bars):** Fixed headers and tab bars use a Soft Linen Tint (`#F2EFEB`) bounded by a subtle 1px hairline divider (`#E8E4DC`), clearly demarcating operational toolbars from scrolling feeds.
- **Active Tactility:** Rather than expanding shadows on interaction, pressable cards and buttons communicate depth through tactile opacity shifts (transitioning to `0.70` - `0.75` on press) and crisp border state transitions to Deep Sage Green (`#2D5A43`).

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary emphasizes friendliness, organic warmth, and ergonomic hand-feel. Geometric sharp edges are strictly avoided.

- **Cards & Dashboard Modules:** Utilize `18px` (large curvature) corner rounding, producing an approachable, human contour that frames local storefront images and workspace metrics softly.
- **Input Fields & Text Areas:** Styled with `14px` (medium curvature) corners for structured, clean touch targets.
- **Buttons, Status Badges & Category Chips:** Employ fully pill-shaped contours (`borderRadius: 999px`) to invite confident, thumb-friendly interaction across mobile viewports.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** Background in Deep Sage Green (`#2D5A43`), text in pure White (`#FFFFFF`), `15px` bold weight, `50px` minimum height, horizontal padding `24px`, fully rounded pill (`999px`).
- **Terracotta Action Button:** Background in Warm Terracotta (`#E06D53`), text in pure White (`#FFFFFF`), used strictly for conversion actions ("Order Now", "Call Store", "Book Stall").
- **Secondary / Linen Pill:** Background in Soft Linen (`#F2EFEB`), label in Charcoal (`#1E2922`), zero border.
- **Ghost Button:** Transparent fill, `15px` bold Deep Sage Green text, no border.

### Chips & Intent Badges
- **Category Filter Chips:** Pill shape with `14px` horizontal and `7px` vertical padding. Inactive state: Surface White with 1px `#E8E4DC` border and Charcoal text. Active state: Solid Deep Sage Green fill with White text.
- **Operational Status Chip:** Pill shape using Soft Mint fill (`#DCFCE7`) paired with Forest Green text (`#15803D`) and `12px` bold type, indicating "Open Now" or "In Stock".
- **Customer Intent Badges:** Domain tags (Food, Kirana, Services, Stalls) render as tinted circular avatars with distinct intent-palette highlights and bold captions.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- **Text Inputs:** Height `48px`, background Surface White (`#FFFFFF`), border 1px solid `#E8E4DC`, corner radius `14px`. Text in Charcoal `16px` to prevent viewport zooming.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts cleanly to Deep Sage Green (`#2D5A43`) with an optional 2px soft sage halo (`#E7EFEA`).
- **Field Labels:** Left-aligned above the input, `14px` medium weight in Charcoal (`#1E2922`). Helper/error text sits directly below in `12px`.

### Cards & Business Listings
- **Neighborhood Business Card:** White background, 1px `#E8E4DC` border, `18px` rounded corners, `16px` internal padding. Top row contains merchant avatar, verified check, and name in `18px` bold. Second row displays the gold star rating cluster (`#F0A500`) and distance tag (`0.8 km away`). Bottom row contains intent tags and a terracotta or sage trigger button.

### Checkboxes, Radios & Switches
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `20px` dimensions, 1.5px border in `#E8E4DC`. Selected state transitions to solid Deep Sage Green fill with a crisp white checkmark or radio dot.
- **Segmented Mode Switcher:** Recessed track in `#F2EFEB` with a sliding pill in crisp Surface White (`#FFFFFF`) and charcoal label.

### Navigation Chrome
- **Header:** Flat, unbordered header utilizing linen paper tint (`#F2EFEB`) or canvas `#FBF9F5` displaying current neighborhood drop-down and line-icon action buttons.
- **Bottom Tab Bar:** Flat `#F2EFEB` background bounded by a 1px top hairline divider (`#E8E4DC`). Active tab encapsulates its icon within a Deep Sage Green pill container (`56px × 30px`, radius `999px`) featuring a white icon.
