SECTION 3 — FULL DESCRIPTION  (4000 char max)
===============================================================================
2526 / 4000 — room to add if you want.

One Place is the local directory for everything around you — the shops, the services, the people
renting things out, and the neighbours selling what they no longer need.

Open it and you see what is actually near you, sorted by distance. No feed, no algorithm deciding
what deserves your attention. Just your neighbourhood.


FIND WHAT IS AROUND YOU

• Browse by what you need — food, health, home services, rentals, electronics and more
• Search shops, dishes, services and items by name
• See real distances and opening hours before you go
• Open a real street map and see everything plotted around you
• Scan a shop's QR code to open its page instantly


DEAL WITH BUSINESSES DIRECTLY

• Order products or a meal, and see the bill before you pay
• Book an appointment for a service
• Message a business and get an answer from a real person
• Call a business inside the app — no phone number is exchanged, in either direction
• Keep every order and bill in one place instead of scattered across chats


A STALL FOR YOUR OWN THINGS

Selling a phone, a bicycle, furniture you no longer need? Put it up in minutes with a photo and a
price. Everything you sell lives in one stall under your name.

Buyers ask questions and make offers on a public thread under each item, so the next person to
wonder the same thing finds it already answered.


RUN YOUR BUSINESS FROM YOUR PHONE

Listing a business is free and takes a few minutes.

• Publish your menu, services, products or rentals with prices
• Take orders and issue bills from your phone
• Answer customer chats and calls, or hand that to your team
• Add managers and staff, and control what each of them can see
• Run monthly plans — a gym membership, a tuition batch, a bus seat
• Show your work in a photo and video showcase
• Get honest ratings from customers who actually bought something


LIVE VEHICLE TRACKING

If you run vehicles — a school bus, a delivery van, a goods truck — your drivers can share their
live position for a shift. The business sees the whole fleet. A parent sees only the bus their
child is on. Drivers switch it on and off themselves, and it is off by default.


BUILT HONESTLY

• Only verified customers can leave a rating, so scores mean something
• Your phone number is never shown to a business unless you become their customer
• No advertising trackers, and no selling your data to anyone
• Browse the whole directory without an account
• Delete your account from inside the app, whenever you want

Made in India, for Indian neighbourhoods.

  FORMATTING: Play renders almost no markup. Use CAPITALS for headings (not #),
  and the bullet character. No emoji — they render inconsistently across
  Android versions.

SECTION 6 — DATA SAFETY
Full version with per-claim code citations: docs/play-store/data-safety.md
===============================================================================

OPENING QUESTIONS
  Does the app collect or share user data?       YES
  Is all data encrypted in transit?              YES
  Can users request data deletion?               YES (in-app AND web URL)

  Encryption justification: every call is HTTPS/WSS. Supabase is HTTPS-only.
  Call media is WebRTC, which is DTLS-SRTP encrypted by mandate. There is no
  cleartext endpoint in the app.

  "Shared" means transferred to a THIRD PARTY. Supabase, LiveKit and Expo are
  service providers acting on our instructions, which Google's own guidance
  says is not sharing. So everything below is COLLECTED: YES, SHARED: NO.

PERSONAL INFO
  Name              Collected. Optional. App functionality.
  User IDs          Collected. Required. App functionality + account management.
                    (The username is the sign-in identifier and is public.)
  Email address     Collected. Optional. App functionality.
                    (A contact detail, never a credential, never verified.
                     Also returned by "Continue with Google".)
  Phone number      Collected. Optional. App functionality.
  Other personal info  Collected. Optional. App functionality.
                    *** DO NOT OMIT THIS ONE. *** A tracked child's label on a
                    school-vehicle run ("Aarav — Grade 3"), and an enrollee name
                    on a membership taken out for someone else. Entered by an
                    adult about another person. It is the answer most likely to
                    be judged inaccurate if left out.

  NOT collected: address, race/ethnicity, political or religious beliefs,
  sexual orientation.

FINANCIAL INFO — NOTHING. No payment instrument ever enters the app. There is
  no gateway; money settles in the real world and a business marks the bill
  paid by hand. Orders and bills are declared under App activity instead.

LOCATION
  Approximate location  Collected. Optional. App functionality.
  Precise location      Collected. Optional. App functionality.
                        Two uses, described separately in SECTION 8:
                        (a) foreground — nearby search and distance sorting;
                        (b) background — ONLY a driver who switched on live
                            sharing for a shift.
  Retention note: one row per driver per business holds the LATEST position
  only, replaced on each update. No trail, no history.

PHOTOS AND VIDEOS
  Photos   Collected. Optional. App functionality. (Item photos and business
           display pictures — public once on a listing.)
  Videos   NOT collected. The showcase stores a LINK to a video hosted
           elsewhere; the app never uploads a video file.

AUDIO
  Voice or sound recordings   Collected. Optional. App functionality.
  *** TICK "PROCESSED EPHEMERALLY". *** Call audio is streamed live and is
  never recorded, stored or written anywhere. The calls table holds metadata
  only. This is the distinction between a calling app and a recording app.

MESSAGES
  Other in-app messages   Collected. Optional. App functionality.
  (Customer chat, business-to-business chat, and the PUBLIC question/offer
   threads on stall items.)
  NOT collected: emails, SMS/MMS.

APP ACTIVITY
  Other user-generated content   Collected. (Listings, menus, reviews.)
  Other actions                  Collected. (Orders, bookings, bills,
                                 memberships and payments.)
  NOT collected: app interactions/analytics, search history, installed apps,
  browsing history. THERE IS NO ANALYTICS SDK IN THE PROJECT — verified against
  package.json: no Firebase Analytics, Sentry, Segment, Amplitude, Mixpanel or
  Facebook SDK.

DEVICE OR OTHER IDS
  Device or other IDs   Collected. Optional. App functionality.
  (1) Push token — used solely to ring incoming calls and deliver alerts.
  (2) expo-updates install ID — over-the-air update checks.

  *** ADVERTISING ID: NO. *** The app does not read the GAID, has no ad SDK,
  and does not track users across apps or sites. The "Sponsored" cards are
  local businesses paying to be featured inside One Place itself; impression
  and tap counts are reported to that business only, never used for profiling.

DATA HANDLING
  Encrypted in transit?              Yes
  Users can request deletion?        Yes
  Collection required to use app?    NO — browsing, search, the map and
                                     listings all work signed out.
  Families policy?                   NO — target audience is 18+.

SECTION 7 — ACCOUNT DELETION  (App content > Data safety)
Full version: docs/play-store/account-deletion.md
===============================================================================

Play requires BOTH routes. Both exist.

  IN-APP    Account tab > "Delete my account" > type your username to confirm.
            Immediate and irreversible. src/app/delete-account.tsx
  WEB URL   https://YOUR-DOMAIN/legal/delete-account.html
            Must load with no login.

  Deletes all data?  Answer ALL DATA, and rely on the disclosed retention.
  Play accepts retained data with a stated legitimate reason (another party's
  records, accounting). Answering "some data" invites a question you would
  answer with the same paragraphs anyway.

WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENS (supabase/migrations/0019_account_deletion.sql):
  DELETED     sign-in, phone, email, saved places, push tokens, location
              shares, own alerts, tracked items (they carry a child's name),
              customer chats both sides, and any listing nobody has used.
  ANONYMISED  orders, bills, bookings, memberships (the business's financial
              records), reviews (a rating is earned from a verified
              transaction), public stall threads. Name becomes "Deleted user".
  BLOCKED     A listing with staff, orders, bills, members, reviews, chats,
              calls, tracked items or an ad campaign stops the deletion until
              it is transferred or taken down. The app names each one.

  Deployed: edge function "delete-account" is ACTIVE on the project.

SECTION 11 — TESTING TRACKS  ** THE 14-DAY WALL **
===============================================================================

INTERNAL TESTING — instant, no review, up to 100 testers. Always first.
  Test on a real device:
    - sign up, sign in, Continue with Google
    - location prompt, nearby businesses load
    - place an order end to end, receive the bill
    - chat message + notification
    - VOICE CALL RINGS WITH THE APP CLOSED  (the FCM path)
    - background location: disclosure > permission > vehicle moves while locked
    - photo upload on a stall item
    - DELETE AN ACCOUNT and verify (account-deletion.md SECTION 7)
    - Privacy Policy link opens the hosted page
    - Dev Tools is NOT visible anywhere

CLOSED TESTING — the scheduling item nobody expects.
  A PERSONAL developer account must run a closed test with roughly 12 testers
  for 14 CONTINUOUS days before it can apply for production access.

  *** VERIFY THE CURRENT NUMBERS IN YOUR OWN CONSOLE — Google has changed them
      more than once (it was 20 for a long stretch). Your console shows the
      rule that applies to you. ***

  - 12 real people with Google accounts who accept the invite AND keep the app
    installed. Someone opting out resets their contribution.
  - The 14 days are continuous. A gap restarts the clock.
  - START IT AS EARLY AS POSSIBLE. Everything else can be done while it runs.

PRODUCTION — after the 14 days, an "Apply for production" button appears.
  Use a STAGED ROLLOUT: 20% first, ramp over a few days.

REVIEW TIMES
  Internal testing            minutes, no review
  First closed-test review    hours to 3 days
  First production review     3-7 days, sometimes 2+ weeks
  Later updates               hours to 2 days
  A new developer account + background location is the slowest combination Play
  has. Assume two weeks. Do not schedule a launch date against it.

SECTION 13 — BLOCKERS. DO NOT SUBMIT UNTIL THESE ARE RESOLVED.
===============================================================================

BLOCKER 1 — NO REPORT OR BLOCK FOR USER-GENERATED CONTENT
  Google Play "User Generated Content" policy. This is the Android twin of the
  Apple 1.2 blocker in ios_description.txt, and it applies just as much — Play
  requires apps hosting UGC to provide an in-app way to REPORT objectionable
  content and to BLOCK abusive users, plus a route for you to act on reports.

  Current state: a codebase search for report / block / moderation tooling
  returns NOTHING. The app has published contact details and no more.

  Affected surfaces: reviews, customer chat, business-to-business chat, the
  public question-and-offer threads on stall items, business listings, and
  user-uploaded stall photos.

  Fix: a report action on reviews, messages, listings and stall items; a
  block-user capability; and somewhere for you to see and action reports (the
  admin console is the obvious home). Not yet written as a prompt — add it to
  prompt.txt as P12.

  Play enforcement is less immediate than Apple's, but this surfaces during the
  content rating questionnaire, where you must answer YES to user-generated
  content. Shipping without it is a live policy violation from day one.

BLOCKER 2 — APP ICON APPEARS TO USE THIRD-PARTY IP
  Google Play Intellectual Property policy.
  The icon pairs "OP" with what appears to be Luffy's straw hat from One Piece,
  a Shueisha/Toei property. (The linked Supabase project is even named "One
  Piece".) Play removes apps for IP infringement on rights-holder complaint,
  and anime rights holders are active in this space. Commission an original
  mark before submitting — far cheaper than a takedown after launch.

NOT A BLOCKER, BUT KNOW ABOUT IT
  - NO PASSWORD RESET EXISTS. Login is username + password, email is an
    unverified contact detail, and nothing was built for recovery. A user who
    forgets their password is locked out permanently. Google sign-in accounts
    are unaffected. Not a Play issue; a support issue from day one.
  - A REVIEWER OUTSIDE INDIA SEES AN EMPTY APP. This is a location-based
    directory with listings only in Indore. Play reviewers are less likely than
    Apple's to fail you for it, but consider seeding a few listings near
    wherever review happens, or make the empty state explain itself.
  - iOS is dropped. app.json still carries ios.bundleIdentifier and
    UIBackgroundModes. Harmless — leave them.
